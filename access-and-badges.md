# Access and badges

| Badge | Who | Gets you into |
|---|---|---|
| Grey | Visitors | Reception, the staff bar (accompanied) |
| Blue | Staff | Offices, the studio, the gallery |
| Indigo | Research | Labs 1–3 |
| None issued | — | Lab 4 |

- Badges don't open Lab 4. Don't ask how Lab 4 is opened.
- Lost badges are reported to Security at night (ext. 0001) and to Reception by day.
- Tailgating is a disciplinary matter, however nicely you hold the door.

The store cupboard is on a combination lock. Facilities hold the combination. Please stop guessing it: the lock keeps count.
