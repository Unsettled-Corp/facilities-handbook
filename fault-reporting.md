# Reporting a fault

Email Facilities with the room, what's wrong, and when you noticed. A photo helps. A photo taken from a chair does not.

## Lighting

The main room's fittings are serviced quarterly. Fitters label each fitting on its underside, so the reference can be read from the floor without a ladder. Please don't try to read it any other way.

Bulbs in the safe room flicker if left on unattended. This is known. Switch them off after use.

## Noise

The safe room is fitted with sound masking. If it seems quiet, that is the masking working. If it seems silent, report it.

## Home Screens and widgets

Not a Facilities matter. Contact IT Security.
