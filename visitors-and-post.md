# Visitors and post

- Visitors sign in at Reception and wear a grey badge at all times.
- Visitors announce themselves at the safe room door the usual way. The door is glass. Breakages are charged to the host's department.
- Parcels are held at Reception and released only against their tracking number. Reception does not keep a record of tracking numbers.
- We never ask for payment to redeliver post. If a message asks you to pay to receive a parcel, delete it.
