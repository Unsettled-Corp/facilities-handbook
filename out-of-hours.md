# Out of hours

Night Security (Owen Pryce, ext. 0001) covers the building from 19:00 to 07:00.

- The building looks different at night. Signage in some corridors is only lit after dark.
- Leave your phone's volume up if you're working late. Security may need to reach you.
- If someone you don't recognise is in the building, don't follow them. Note what you can and call ext. 0001.
